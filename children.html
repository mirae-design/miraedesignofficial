CHILDREN'S PRODUCT

"IT IS SAFE FOR BABIES AND CHILDREN."

1-2 months

Wash the baby bottles beforehand, spray them with the spray before use, wait a little while, and then rinse.

3-4 months

I sprayed and wiped down everything I could reach, including pacifiers, toys, baby carriers, chairs, floors, and train handrails.

5 months

I sprayed this on clothes that had leaked and soiled with feces, then hand-washed them with detergent. It removed both the stain and the smell very well, making the process much easier.

BABY BOTTLE

01

Disassemble the baby bottle and rinse it lightly with water.

02

Pour undiluted e-WASH into the pot.

03

Immerse all the washed parts in the pot.

04

Heat the pot until small bubbles appear around the edges, then turn off the heat.

05

Leave all the parts submerged in the e-WASH in the pot for 5 to 10 minutes.

06

Remove all parts from the pot, rinse them lightly with water, and then wipe off any excess moisture.
